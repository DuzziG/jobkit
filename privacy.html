Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

JobKit is a job-search tool that runs on your own computer. There is no JobKit service in the cloud: no account with us, no server of ours, no analytics. This page says what JobKit does with your data, and with data from your Google account if you connect it.

Where your data lives

Your CV, applications, notes and settings are stored in a database on your own computer. They are not sent to the makers of JobKit, and nobody but you can see them.

Data from Google

Connecting Google is optional. If you connect it, JobKit asks for these permissions, and uses each only as described:

PermissionWhat JobKit does with it
Create Gmail drafts (gmail.compose) Writes application emails as drafts, with your CV and letter attached. JobKit has no feature that sends email: you review and send each draft yourself.
Read Gmail (gmail.readonly) Finds job-alert emails from job sites (such as LinkedIn and Indeed) and keeps the jobs listed in them: title, company, place, link, and the date the email arrived. No other email is read or kept.
Calendar events (calendar.events) Adds, moves and removes the events for your interviews.
Your email address (openid, email) Shows which Google account is connected.

JobKit keeps the sign-in from Google (a refresh token) in the database on your computer. It never sells data from Google, never uses it for advertising, and never shares it with anyone, except as below.

The AI you choose

If you turn on an AI provider in JobKit's settings (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Mistral) and use a button that asks it to write, JobKit sends that provider what the task needs: your CV and the job posting, which may have come from a job-alert email. That provider's own privacy policy applies. You can use JobKit without one.

JobKit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect Google in JobKit's Connections screen removes the sign-in from your computer and revokes it at Google.
  • You can also remove JobKit's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Deleting JobKit's data folder on your computer deletes everything JobKit stored.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page will show the new version and its date. Questions are welcome in the project's issues.
