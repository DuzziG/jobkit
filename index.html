JobKit

A personal job-search tool that runs on your own computer.

JobKit keeps one CV up to date, writes a tailored CV and cover letter for each job without inventing experience, tracks every application, and shows what gets replies. An AI assistant can do everything in it that you can.

Google

If you connect your Google account, JobKit:

  • writes application emails as drafts in Gmail, with your CV and letter attached. You send them yourself: JobKit never sends email;
  • reads job-alert emails from job sites such as LinkedIn and Indeed, and keeps only the jobs listed in them;
  • adds your interviews to Google Calendar.

Everything stays on your computer. See the privacy policy.
